# 1. PRODUCT VISION

Christ: The Scroll is not intended to be another generic Bible reader.

It is not intended to compete with existing Bible applications primarily by offering:

* more Bible versions
* more devotionals
* more reading plans
* generic AI chat
* another social network
* another collection of commentaries

Its defining purpose is:

> **Turn every passage of Scripture into an interactive doorway into the people, places, events, geography, historical setting, timeline, related Scripture, and larger biblical story surrounding that passage.**

The core product journey is:

SCRIPTURE
↓
UNDERSTAND
↓
EXPLORE
↓
PEOPLE
PLACES
EVENTS
TIMELINE
RELATED SCRIPTURE
CONTEXT
↓
STORY
↓
CONTINUE

The experience should make users think:

> "I have read this verse before, but I have never explored it this way."

---

# 2. FUNDAMENTAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

The following rules are NON-NEGOTIABLE.

## Principle 1 — Scripture Is Primary

Technology must make Scripture easier to encounter and understand.

Technology must never replace Scripture.

The biblical text must always be visually and technically distinguished from:

* AI explanations
* commentary
* historical context
* reflections
* summaries
* translations
* educational material

Always use visible content labels such as:

SCRIPTURE

AI EXPLANATION

HISTORICAL CONTEXT

RELATED SCRIPTURE

INTERPRETATION

REFLECTION

AI-ASSISTED TRANSLATION

Never merge Scripture and AI-generated content into one unlabeled text block.

---

## Principle 2 — Exploration Is the Differentiator

Every passage should potentially connect with:

* people
* places
* events
* timeline periods
* journeys
* related passages
* historical context
* themes

A verse is not merely something to read.

It becomes an entry point into the biblical world.

---

## Principle 3 — Simplicity First

The application may contain deep information, but the interface must remain simple.

The default experience should expose only three major actions:

READ

UNDERSTAND

EXPLORE

Advanced complexity should appear only after the user requests it.

---

## Principle 4 — AI Must Be Grounded

Do not create a generic unrestricted Bible chatbot.

AI responses should be retrieval-first.

Preferred pipeline:

USER QUESTION
↓
CURRENT PASSAGE
↓
RETRIEVE SCRIPTURE
↓
RETRIEVE RELATED VERSES
↓
RETRIEVE CURATED PEOPLE/PLACE/EVENT DATA
↓
RETRIEVE HISTORICAL METADATA
↓
GENERATE RESPONSE
↓
DISPLAY SOURCES/REFERENCES

Do not rely solely on the language model's memory for questions that can be grounded in stored Scripture and curated app data.
